MindBridge – Student Mental Wellness Platform
About the Project
MindBridge is a digital mental wellness platform designed to support college students in managing stress and expressing their emotions in a safe and supportive environment.

Problem Statement
Many college students face academic pressure and emotional difficulties but hesitate to seek help because of social stigma and fear of judgment.

Objectives
Encourage students to check in on their emotional well-being.
Provide accessible initial mental wellness support.
Help students find appropriate counselling resources.
Promote awareness of mental health among college students.
Key Features
Student wellness check-ins
AI-assisted support
Counselling access information
Student-friendly and accessible interface
